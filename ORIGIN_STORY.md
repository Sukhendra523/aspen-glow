# The Origin Story of Aspen Glow CMS 💅🤖

> **Didi Shock 😯 · Me Rock 😁 · AI Be Like 😭**\
> *"Meri shaktiyon ka galat istemal ho raha hai!"*

## The Problem: Festival Season = Poster Season

Every festival, the same story began.

Diwali? Poster chahiye.\
Teej? Poster chahiye.\
Karwa Chauth? Poster chahiye.\
Wedding season? **Bhai, ek aur poster bana do!** 😂

My sister runs **Aspen Glow by Shilpi**, a beauty parlour. Naturally,
she wanted beautiful promotional posters for her services and festive
offers.

And naturally, she had one highly convenient in-house resource:

**Her brother --- a Software Engineer.**

I tried explaining the basic facts of life:

> "Didi, main Software Engineer hoon, Graphic Designer nahi.\
> Code likhwa lo, application banwa lo... lekin har festival ka poster
> mujhse mat banwao. Please, mera time waste mat karo!" 😭

But didi was not accepting this technical limitation. Her requirements
were clear, her festive calendar was unstoppable, and her poster
requests had **production-level persistence**.

## Phase 1: ChatGPT to the Rescue... Again... and Again... and Again

Fine. I decided to use AI.

I asked ChatGPT to design posters according to her requirements. It
helped me create festive creatives, package layouts, promotional copy,
and all the shiny beauty-parlour vibes.

One poster became two. Two became many. Every new festival brought a new
design request.

I was using AI's *super shakti* again and again just to survive the next
poster deadline.

**AI was powerful. The poster requests were even more powerful.**

Eventually, I reached the final boss level:

> "Bas! Ab aur poster nahi banaunga. Is problem ka permanent solution
> banana padega." 😤

## Phase 2: Software Engineer Mode --- ACTIVATED ⚡

Then came the legendary idea:

**Why design every poster manually when I can build a CMS that lets didi
create them herself?**

So I opened my editor, called upon Gemini --- *Argon ki super shakti* 🚀
--- and started building the **Aspen Glow CMS**.

Instead of repeatedly creating individual posters, I built a portal
where my sister can manage the business content herself.

The CMS includes:

-   **Brand & Contact Settings** --- manage the salon's name, logo,
    colours, phone number, WhatsApp number, and address.
-   **Service Catalogue** --- add, edit, and manage beauty services and
    prices.
-   **Package Management** --- create service packages with
    descriptions, prices, and inclusions.
-   **Bundle Management** --- combine packages into promotional offers.
-   **Media Library** --- manage approved logos and visual assets.
-   **Festival Management** --- configure festival names, headlines,
    themes, and campaign details.
-   **Festival Poster Studio** --- select packages or bundles, choose a
    festival and template, customise the copy, preview the design, save
    drafts, and export posters as PNGs.

In other words, I replaced a recurring family request with a reusable
software solution.

**One CMS. Multiple festivals. Fewer emergency poster requests.** 😎

## Phase 3: The Final Result

Now didi can update her offers and generate festive posters herself,
without waiting for her brother to become a part-time graphic designer.

  Character   Reaction
  ----------- -------------------------------------------------------
  👩‍🎨 Didi     **"Wait... ab poster main khud bana sakti hoon?"** 😯
  👨‍💻 Me       **"Didi Shock. Me Rock."** 😁
  🤖 AI       **"Meri shaktiyon ka galat istemal ho raha hai!"** 😭
  💻 CMS      **"Request automated successfully."** ✅

The real win? My sister gets a tool tailored to her business, and I get
to focus on software engineering instead of becoming the family's
unofficial festival-poster department.

## The Moral of the Story

> **If a task keeps coming back, don't just repeat it --- automate it.**

A little sibling chaos, a lot of AI assistance, and some software
engineering turned a recurring poster problem into a proper CMS.

Built with love for **Aspen Glow by Shilpi**...\
...and with a very strong desire to protect my free time. 😂❤️
